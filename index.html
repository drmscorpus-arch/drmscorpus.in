Our first year

Where we began

Dr MS Corpus began with a simple belief: medical education doesn’t have to be this hard. Not because medicine is easy, because it never will be, but because the right strategies, support and mindset let you thrive instead of just survive.

It started as one doctor’s personal journey of late nights, imposter syndrome, and the search for a better way to understand. Then it became a family mission. My parents, my brother and I decided to build it together, as equal partners. So when you read “I” anywhere on this platform, it truly means “we.”

We never wanted to be another source of dry lectures and impossible jargon. We wanted to write the way a good senior explains things: clinically, clearly, and with the story behind the facts.

“Medicine isn’t a destination. It’s an evolving journey, and we’re all students for life.”

Our year in numbers

13 articles, 1 printable PAC checklist, 50 YouTube subscribers, 28 Instagram followers, 4 founders and one family. These are not big numbers, and we’re proud of them anyway. Every subscriber and follower is a real person who chose to learn alongside us when we had almost nothing to show.

We’ll be plain about the rest, too: in this first year we made just one handout, and our inbox stayed quiet. That’s our starting line, and year two is about making more handouts and starting more conversations with you.

What we built

Anaesthesiology: PAC: The Anaesthesiologist’s OPD, PONV Prophylaxis, Volatile Anaesthetics, The Three Minutes That Matter, Pulse Oximetry, Hyperkalemia: The Silent ECG Killer, What Is a Mechanical Ventilator, Really?, Scalp Block: My First Time, Spinal Anaesthesia.

Pharmacology: Propofol: The Milk of Amnesia, Etomidate: Guardian of Hemodynamic Stability, Thiopentone, Ondansetron: The Anti-Emetic Explained.

Handout: PAC Checklist: The Resident’s Proforma, a printable pre-anaesthetic evaluation checklist for residents and students.

An honest note

We went quiet. After a promising start, the posts slowed and then stopped. If you checked in and found nothing new, you deserved an explanation, and we owe you at least an apology.

Building something alongside studies, work and family life is harder than it looks from the outside. What changed: we stopped aiming for perfect and started aiming for consistent. Smaller pieces, a realistic rhythm, and a plan we can actually keep. We’re back, starting on our first anniversary.

What’s next

  • Handouts: printable checklists and one-page summaries for the OT, the ward and the exam hall.
  • Blogs: clinical stories and concept deep-dives in anaesthesiology and pharmacology.
  • Videos and shorts: pearls, walkthroughs and how-we-use-it demos on YouTube and Instagram.
  • Your topics: you decide what we cover next.

Tell us what to make next: reply on Instagram, comment on YouTube, or write to drmscorpus@gmail.com. Every message is read by our family team.
